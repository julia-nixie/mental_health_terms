How to read this

Source: Google Books Ngram Viewer, English corpus, 1800–2022. A value is the share of all phrases of that length in the year's books, shown per million.

Each group adds together its listed variants and their plural and spelling forms, for example "autism", "autistic" and "autists". Abbreviations were matched in capitals only.

Frequencies show how often words are used in print, not how common a condition is. Some words, such as "retarded" and "dementia", were in everyday use long before and after they were used as diagnoses.
